## The problem

The goal was to raise the share of first-time mutual fund investors on Groww who still have an active SIP twelve months after starting. Before deciding what to build, I wanted to know why SIPs stop, in investors' own words.

## What I found

I collected 7,600 public posts from app stores, Reddit, YouTube and forums, narrowed them to 2,586 about SIPs, 443 about stopping, and finally **219 first-person stories**. AI coded them, and I checked its coding against 20 posts I had hand-labelled blind.

The finding: **many SIPs stop by accident**. A salary comes in late, a payment fails, and three misses in a row cancel the SIP. The lighter options (skip a month, pay less, move the date) already exist, but people find them too late. Six interviews backed this up.

## What I built

**GR-1** puts an AI assistant at the moment a SIP is at risk: a failed payment, a tight month, or a tap on Cancel. A rules engine works out what is actually allowed for that fund and that date. The AI then explains it in plain language and points to the option that fits the moment.

Because this is about people's money, the AI is gated. Every answer passes five code checks and an AI reviewer before anyone sees it.

## How I knew it was ready

I wrote 32 test questions, including the tricky and unsafe ones, and ran the MVP against them seven times, fixing what failed each round. The latest run passed 30 of 32, with **zero unsafe answers in all seven runs**. Three user tests on the live MVP followed.
